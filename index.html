FocusNote Privacy Policy

FocusNote is a simple note-taking app designed to help you capture thoughts, tasks, plans and ideas.

Data collection

FocusNote does not collect, transmit or share personal data. Your notes are stored locally on your device.

Third-party services

FocusNote does not include advertising, analytics, tracking or third-party data-collection services.

Contact

If you have questions about this privacy policy, contact: appsbymarg@gmail.com

Support

If you need help with FocusNote, have a question, or want to report a problem, contact: appsbymarg@gmail.com When reporting a problem, please include your iPhone model, iOS version, and a brief description of what happened. Please do not include private note content.
